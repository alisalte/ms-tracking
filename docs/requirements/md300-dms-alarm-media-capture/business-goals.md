# Business Goals

| ID | Goal | Measurable when requirements are complete |
|---|---|---|
| BG-DMS-01 | Every in-scope DMS **and ADAS** alarm on MD300 has a driver still captured at alarm time. | Alarm record can be linked to a JPEG of the cabin/driver for that event. |
| BG-DMS-02 | Every in-scope DMS **and ADAS** alarm on MD300 has a 15 s driver video around alarm time. | Alarm record can be linked to a 5+10 s clip with audio. |
| BG-DMS-03 | Operators can review that photo and clip as evidence of the same alarm (not unrelated nearby files). | Evidence is attributable to that alarm occurrence. |
| BG-DMS-04 | Capture must not depend on the operator being online at the moment of the alarm. | Device (or platform follow-up) captures even if no one has the drawer open. |

Goals are outcomes, not a design of commands or storage.
