# Business goals

Draft — confirm with stakeholder.

1. **Detect** hard brake, overspeed, and idle events with enough fidelity for ops and scoring.
2. **Score** each driver so managers can compare safety performance over a period.
3. **Surface** score and events on a Driver Dashboard and a Ranking view.
4. **Notify** the right people when high-severity behavior occurs or scores drop.
5. **Attach evidence** (video/photo) to relevant events where hardware supports it.
6. **Coach** drivers with actionable guidance (Sprint 3).
7. **Predict** elevated risk so interventions happen before incidents (Sprint 3).

## Non-goals (until confirmed otherwise)

- Replacing regulatory Hours of Service (HOS) compliance.
- Replacing on-device Parameter threshold configuration (separate product area).
