# Business problem

Fleet operators need a consistent way to **detect unsafe or inefficient driving**, **score drivers**, and **act** (rank, notify, coach) — not only configure device thresholds or view raw alarms.

Today in FleetVision:

- Device Parameter can set overspeed / harsh brake thresholds on Meitrack units.
- Alarms / DMS evidence exist in limited form.
- Driver ↔ vehicle assignment is **current only**; alarms are **not reliably attributed** to a driver at event time (see project memory).
- There is **no** shipped Driver Score, ranking, coaching, or risk prediction product surface in the web dashboard.

Without this, safety ops cannot compare drivers, prioritize coaching, or prove improvement over time.
